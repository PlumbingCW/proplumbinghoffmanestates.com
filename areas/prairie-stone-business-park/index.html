Business park and retail district

Commercial Plumber at Prairie Stone and Poplar Creek at 59/90, Hoffman Estates, IL

Prairie Stone Business Park and the Poplar Creek at 59/90 district hold offices, an arena, restaurants and large retail along the north side of I-90. If a restroom, kitchen drain or water heater fails in a building here, call (630) 822-9290 with the address and the problem.

Call (630) 822-9290

What these two districts are

The Village history page describes Prairie Stone as roughly 800 acres planned for office, research and high-tech use. Sears moved its headquarters there in 1992. Today the NOW Arena, a Village-owned 11,000-seat venue, stands at 5333 Prairie Stone Parkway in ZIP 60192.

The Village's own recruitment brochure uses the name Poplar Creek at 59/90 for the retail and entertainment district west of Route 59 and north of I-90. A Village planning document adds that Prairie Stone includes preserved wetlands. The Village's Western Area Plan covers about 1,650 more acres at its western edge, so this part of town is still changing. These are commercial districts, not residential neighborhoods.

Plumbing in offices, an arena district and retail

Large buildings put heavy use on a few fixtures. Restrooms on one supply line, kitchens that run all day and floor drains in back-of-house areas are where a plumber looks first. The plumber starts by finding out which part of the system serves the problem.

A repeated clog in a restroom or kitchen line often needs more than a snake. Hydro jetting scours the pipe wall, and a camera shows what is left behind. For hidden water loss, leak detection can trace a line without opening every wall. Call (630) 822-9290

Who to call first in a leased building

Most tenants do not own the plumbing. The lease decides who handles a shared drain, a roof leak or a water heater serving several units. Before you call, check with your property manager and tell the plumber what the lease says.

Wetlands, digging and permits

Prairie Stone includes preserved wetlands, so ask the property owner before any digging near drainage areas. Call J.U.L.I.E. at 811 as well so buried utilities are marked. That is the first step for sewer line repair or a sewer camera inspection that leads to a dig.

Commercial plumbing work in the Village falls under the Illinois Plumbing Code, which the Village has adopted, and plumbers must hold an Illinois license. Village Development Services, Code Enforcement, answers permit questions at 847-781-2631, and contractors register on the Village's BS&A Online portal.
