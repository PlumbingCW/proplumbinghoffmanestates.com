Retail and redevelopment area

Plumber at Barrington Square and Higgins Road, Hoffman Estates, IL

Barrington Square is a multi-tenant retail center on West Higgins Road, and the Village has treated the surrounding blocks as a redevelopment area since 2012. Shared drains and restaurant kitchens are the usual plumbing questions in centers like this, so call (630) 822-9290 with the unit and the problem.

Call (630) 822-9290

What Barrington Square is

A trade report lists the center at 2300 to 2356 West Higgins Road, with 101,796 square feet on 11 acres. The same report says it sold for $9 million to a local investment group, the first sale of the property. It names a bowling center, a gift shop and café, and an Italian restaurant among the tenants.

In June 2009 the Daily Herald described it as a strip mall on Higgins Road near Barrington Road whose former anchor had closed. On May 14, 2012, the Village board created a 33-acre tax increment financing district around the Barrington Square Town Center. It covers 10 parcels and three buildings, including the mall, and does not extend north of Hassell Road.

Why redevelopment matters for plumbing

The Village's Barrington Road plan covers about 950 acres around the I-90 interchange and names Barrington Square Town Center as a possible site for more residential density. Nothing there is final, and a plan is not a permit. The practical point is that tenant build-outs and remodels around Higgins and Barrington Roads are likely to keep coming.

New walls and new fixtures mean new plumbing. Village permits for plumbing work are based on fixture count in its fee handout, so confirm costs with Village Development Services at 847-781-2631 before you sign a contract. Ask the plumber for a written scope that the permit office can review.

Shared drains in a strip center

In a multi-tenant building, one building drain often serves many units. A clog in a tenant's sink may be a branch line problem. A backup that shows up in several units at once points to the shared main. Sewer camera inspection shows which one it is, and the answer decides who pays.

Restaurants add grease, and grease sticks to pipe walls and narrows them over time. Drain cleaning clears the line for now, and hydro jetting cleans the pipe wall. Ask the property manager and the Village what grease control the building requires before you buy equipment. Call (630) 822-9290

Tenant work, permits and licensed plumbers

Plumbing work here falls under the Illinois Plumbing Code, which the Village adopted, and Illinois requires plumbers to be licensed. The Village asks for a rough plumbing inspection and a final inspection on remodel work. Contractors register on the BS&A Online portal before applying for a permit.

For a failed fixture, a leaking supply line or a floor drain that will not clear, see commercial plumbing. Call (630) 822-9290 with the building, the unit and what you see.
