Start with the water you actually have

Hoffman Estates gets its water from Lake Michigan, purchased from the City of Chicago, and has since 1985. The Village's 2025 Water Quality Report lists chlorine as the disinfectant, averaging 1.2 parts per million, and reports no drinking water quality violations that year. It does not print a hardness figure, so no one can tell you your hardness from the report alone.

That matters because a softener sized for the wrong hardness wastes salt and water. A simple test strip or a lab test from your tap tells you where you stand. Tell us what the test says when you Call (630) 822-9290, and we can size the unit and the plumbing around it.

Softener or filter: which problem are you solving?

A softener swaps hardness minerals for sodium or potassium, which helps with scale on fixtures, spotty glass and soap that does not lather. A filter removes other things: taste and odor from chlorine, sediment, or specific contaminants. Many homes want a softener, a carbon filter or both.

The Village report shows the Chicago supply at 9.10 parts per million sodium at its highest, and it adds fluoride on purpose. A softener adds sodium to soft water, so people on a low-sodium diet should ask a doctor. If your goal is better-tasting water at the kitchen tap, a carbon filter may be a smaller step than a whole-house softener.

Installing a softener or whole-home filter

Installation is plumbing work. We cut into the main supply line, add a bypass valve set so you can isolate the unit, connect the drain line and brine overflow and test the system under pressure. Placement matters too, since the unit needs a drain, a power outlet and room for salt.

Whole-home filter housings go in a similar spot, usually before the water heater. If you already bought a unit, tell us the brand. We also install the shutoff, the drain connection and an air gap where the unit discharges, and we confirm everything with you before leaving.

Softener not working or leaking

A softener that stops softening may have run out of salt, bridged its brine tank, clogged its injector or failed its control valve. A softener that leaks or overflows can be a stuck valve or a drain line that is kinked or blocked. Put the unit on bypass to stop water from flowing through it, and (630) 822-9290 us.

We replace and reinstall valves, bypass assemblies and drain connections. We can also check the plumbing around the unit, since a failing fridge water line valve or a loose connector near the softener sometimes causes the flood that gets blamed on it. If the source is unclear, our leak detection service can trace it.

Quick checks before you call

  • Is there salt in the brine tank, and is it a hard crust or a solid bridge?
  • Is the bypass valve in the service position or the bypass position?
  • Is the unit plugged in, and is the display showing a fault?
  • Is the drain line kinked, frozen or blocked?
  • Where exactly is water collecting, and does it stop on bypass?

Brine tanks and things dropped inside

The brine well is the narrow chamber inside the salt tank. Tools, box cutters and bottle caps do fall into it when people open salt bags. Do not reach in with a long tool while the unit is regenerating. We can retrieve objects and clean out salt mush, then check the float and the valve. (630) 822-9290 if something has fallen in.

If you are buying a home, a softener inspection before closing is a smart step. We can check the valves, the bypass, the drain and the age of the unit. Mention any seller notes when you call.

Lead, water quality and what a filter can and cannot do

The Village states there are no known lead service lines in Hoffman Estates, and its 2025 report shows no sites over the lead action level. Private plumbing inside a house is another matter, and older fixtures can contribute lead. If you have specific concerns, the Village Public Works department offers lead testing, and a lab test of your own tap gives the clearest picture.

A filter does not fix every problem, and we do not claim it does. We help you pick a unit rated for what you want to reduce, then install it so it works as designed.
