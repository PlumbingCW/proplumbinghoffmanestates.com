About Hoffman Estates Pro Plumbing

Hoffman Estates Pro Plumbing is a call-only plumbing dispatch line for Hoffman Estates, IL and nearby towns. You call, describe the problem, and dispatch arranges a licensed and insured plumber.

Call (630) 822-9290

What we are

This site is a phone line, not a showroom. There are no forms to fill out and no accounts to create. Everything starts with a call to (630) 822-9290.

The line is staffed 24/7, including nights, weekends and holidays, because plumbing problems do not keep office hours. The mailing address is 535 W Higgins Rd, Hoffman Estates, IL 60169. Call before you visit so dispatch can tell you who is on site.

How a call works

Dispatch asks what is happening, where you are, and how urgent it is. Tell them about any active water, a drain backing up, or any smell. If you can safely reach your main water shutoff, they will tell you when to close it.

Next, dispatch matches your job to a plumber who serves your address. You hear the price before work starts. Same-day appointments are available when schedules allow.

Safety comes first

If you smell gas or suspect carbon monoxide, leave the building and call your gas utility or 911 before you call us. Do not touch electrical equipment near standing water.

Where we work

We cover Hoffman Estates, including ZIP codes 60169, 60192, 60179 and 60195, and the neighboring village of Schaumburg. Calls from nearby towns such as Streamwood, Roselle and Palatine are welcome too. See the service area page for details.

What to have ready before you call

A few details speed up the call and help dispatch send the right plumber with the right parts. None of this is required, and dispatch will ask about anything you forget.

  • The address and a callback number. Dispatch needs to know the street, the unit or floor, and a number that stays on.
  • What you see, hear or smell. Running water, a slow drain, a gurgle in the toilet, a smell of sewage or a pilot light that will not stay lit are all useful clues.
  • When it started. A leak that began this morning is a different job from a drip that has grown for a month.
  • Whether you can reach the shutoff. If you know where the main valve is, say so. If you do not, dispatch will talk you through finding it.
  • Photos, if you can take them safely. A picture of the water heater label or the wet ceiling helps, and it also helps with an insurance claim later.

What counts as an emergency

Call right away if water is running and will not stop, a drain is backing up into the home, a pipe has burst, or a water heater is leaking or has no hot water in cold weather. Sewage coming up through a floor drain is also urgent because it carries a health risk.

Some jobs can wait for a scheduled visit. A slow drip under a sink, a toilet that runs after flushing or a faucet that sprays sideways are good examples. Even so, small leaks tend to grow, so it is worth calling soon.

If you are not sure which kind of problem you have, call anyway. Dispatch will tell you honestly whether it needs a night visit or whether a morning appointment is fine.

How pricing works

You hear the price before work starts. The price depends on what is actually wrong, how hard the part is to reach, what materials the job needs and the time of day. A clogged kitchen drain and a collapsed sewer line sit at very different ends of that range, so there is no single number to quote in advance.

If the plumber finds something different from what you described, you are told before any extra work begins. You can say yes, say no, or ask for a day to think it over when safety allows.

What this site does not do

This site does not sell products, take online bookings or collect your details through a form. It does not publish reviews or ratings, and it does not claim awards or rankings. It describes how to reach the dispatch line and what to expect when you call.

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. The privacy policy explains what that means, and the terms of use cover the rest.
