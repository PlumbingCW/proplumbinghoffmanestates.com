Emergency Burst Pipe Repair in Hoffman Estates, IL

A burst pipe can put a lot of water into a house in a short time, so stop the water first and call second. Our line answers 24/7. The steps below protect your floors, your electrical system and your insurance claim.

Call (630) 822-9290

What to do right now

Do these in order. Do not stop to mop or to hunt for a towel until the water is off.

Burst pipe steps

  • Shut off the water. Turn the main valve clockwise until it stops. It is usually near the water meter, in the basement or utility room, or where the line enters the house. If you cannot find it or it will not turn, call us and say so.
  • Cut power if water is near electricity. If water is dripping through a light fixture or pooling around outlets, switch off the breaker only if you can reach the panel without stepping in water. Otherwise stay out and call 911.
  • Open the lowest faucet. Run it to drain what is left in the pipes, which also eases pressure on the break.
  • Take photos and video. Capture the broken pipe, the wet walls, floors and belongings before you move anything. Insurers ask for these.
  • Move what you can. Lift rugs, electronics and papers out of the wet area.

When a burst pipe is an emergency

Any pipe that is actively spraying or running is an emergency. So is a pipe that has split inside a wall, even if you cannot see the water. A slow drip is urgent but not always a middle-of-the-night call.

If you are unsure, call (630) 822-9290 and describe what you see. The plumber will tell you whether to wait for morning or whether to send someone tonight. We take calls at night, on weekends and on holidays.

Frozen pipes and winter breaks in Hoffman Estates

Winter is the main cause of burst pipes in this part of Illinois. Water expands as it freezes, and the pipe splits where the pressure builds, usually just past the ice plug. The damage often shows only after the ice melts and water starts to flow.

Village code puts the frost line at 42 inches, which tells you how deep the cold reaches. NWS data for O'Hare shows a January average low of 18.8 F, and the lowest daily normal low is 17.9 F in late January. Pipes in exterior walls, garages, crawlspaces and unheated basements are the ones at risk.

If a pipe is frozen but not yet burst, keep the water on at a trickle, open the cabinet doors below the sink and warm the area gently. Never use an open flame. When the thawed pipe shows a crack, shut the main and call.

Cracked or broken pipe repair options

Not every burst pipe needs the same fix. The plumber looks at the pipe material, the size of the split and how much of the line is weak. The choice then comes down to speed, cost and how long you want the repair to last.

A single split in copper pipe is often a quick spot repair. A pipe that has failed twice in a year is telling you something else is wrong. In that case, repiping is worth a conversation.

Copper, PEX and other pipe materials

Copper is common in older homes and holds up well, but it can split when frozen or pit from the inside over time. Plastic piping such as PEX tolerates freezing better, though fittings can still fail. The plumber identifies what you have before choosing a repair.

Mixed materials call for the right connectors. Joining dissimilar metals without the right fitting can cause corrosion later. That is one reason a licensed plumber matters here. To talk through your pipe type, call (630) 822-9290.

Broken supply valves and fixture lines

A burst under a sink, behind a toilet or at a washing machine is usually a supply valve or braided line, not the main pipe. These fail more often than most people expect and can flood a room fast. The fix is often a new valve or line, and it can be done at the same visit.

If a valve will not shut off, close the main and call. For a leaking faucet or toilet that is not flooding, see faucet and sink repair.

Documenting the damage for insurance

Your photos and video matter. Keep the broken section of pipe if the plumber removes it, because an adjuster may ask to see it. Write down the time you noticed the break and when you shut off the water.

The plumber can give you a written description of the cause and the repair. Water cleanup and drywall are separate trades, and your insurer may have preferred vendors. Call (630) 822-9290 if you need a plumber's note for the claim.

Permits and the Illinois Plumbing Code

Emergency repairs can start right away in Hoffman Estates. The Village asks that a permit be submitted within 24 hours after work begins. Its fee schedule lists a plumbing permit at $75 plus $10 per fixture, and a sewer or water service repair at $75.

All plumbing work follows the Illinois Plumbing Code, 77 Ill. Adm. Code 890, which the Village adopted. A licensed plumber handles the paperwork. If the burst is on the buried line from the street, our water service line repair page covers the extra steps.

According to that code, new pipe and fittings are judged on materials and joints, not on whether the leak has stopped. A clamp or push-on fitting that holds tonight is often a stopgap, and the permanent repair is done once the pipe is open and dry.
