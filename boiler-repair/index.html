Boiler Repair in Hoffman Estates, IL

Boiler repair in Hoffman Estates starts with safety. If you smell gas, leave the building and call the Nicor Gas emergency line or 911 from outside. For leaks, pressure problems, strange noises or no heat, call (630) 822-9290 and we will help you sort out what to do next.

Gas smell or carbon monoxide: do this first

A boiler burns fuel, so a gas smell or a carbon monoxide alarm is not a plumbing inconvenience. It is a reason to leave. Walk everyone, including pets, out of the building. Do not switch lights on or off, use lighters or try to find the source.

From outside, call the Nicor Gas emergency line or 911. Wait for the gas company or the fire department to say the home is safe. Then call (630) 822-9290 and we will look at the boiler. We never ask you to repair a gas boiler, a vent or a gas line yourself. Call (630) 822-9290

A leaking boiler

Water around a boiler is one of the more common problems. It can come from a loose fitting, a weeping valve, a failed circulator seal, a cracked heat exchanger or a pressure relief valve that is discharging. Some of these are minor repairs, while a leak from the boiler body is a bigger issue.

Turn off the boiler at its switch and, if you can reach it, close the water feed to the unit. Keep the area dry and away from electrical equipment. Take a photo of where the water appears and the pressure gauge. That tells us a lot before we arrive.

A leak that seems to come from the boiler but is really a pipe nearby is common too. When the source is unclear, our leak detection service can trace it. A burst or frozen pipe in the heating loop may call for burst pipe repair. Call (630) 822-9290

Pressure problems

A hot water boiler runs at a set pressure, shown on a gauge on the unit. If the gauge drops, there may be a leak somewhere in the system. If it climbs too high, the relief valve may open, which can mean a failed expansion tank or an overfeeding fill valve.

Do not keep topping up a system that is losing pressure without finding out why. Fresh water brings in new oxygen and minerals, which speeds up corrosion inside the boiler and pipes. We find the cause first, and then fix it.

No heat, or uneven heat

When the house is cold, a boiler may not be firing, or it may be firing while the heat is not getting where it should. A tripped safety control, a failed circulator, a bad zone valve or a stuck thermostat can each leave you cold. Air trapped in the radiators or baseboards is another frequent cause.

Noises, smells and short cycling

Kettle-like rumbling, banging and gurgling usually point to scale, sediment or air in the system. A boiler that starts, runs a minute and shuts off is short cycling, which wears parts out quickly. A burning or musty smell deserves a check as well, because it can point to a vent or burner problem.

Do not ignore soot around the boiler, a yellow or lazy flame, or a carbon monoxide alarm. Those are warning signs that combustion is not clean. Shut the boiler off if you can do so safely and call us. (630) 822-9290

Boilers and hot water in Hoffman Estates

Winters here are cold, and the Village's building code sets the frost line depth at 42 inches. In practice, that means supply lines, exterior hose bibs and any pipe in an unheated space can freeze if heat fails while you are away. The Village's public works guidance is to keep your thermostat at 50 degrees Fahrenheit or higher when you are gone.

If a boiler fails during a cold snap, take the frozen pipe risk seriously. Open cabinet doors on exterior walls and keep the water moving slightly in exposed lines. Call early, because a no-heat problem is easier to fix before a pipe bursts. Our 24/7 line answers all hours.

Some homes have a boiler that also heats domestic hot water. If yours does, a failure may leave you without heat and without hot showers. If you also have a tank, our water heater repair page covers that side.

What a service visit includes

We start by asking questions about the age, the fuel, the symptoms and what changed. On site, we check pressure, the water level or feed, the circulator, the zone valves, the expansion tank and the relief valve. We look at the burner and vent only as far as our license and the situation allow, and tell you clearly when a manufacturer or specialist is the right call. Call (630) 822-9290

If a part has failed, we explain the cost and wait for your approval. If the boiler is at the end of its life, we say so plainly and go over options. We would rather you know early than pay for several repairs on a unit that will not last.

Boiler work is judged against the manufacturer's installation instructions, which are the standard for clearances, vent connections and pressure settings. That is why we match a replacement part to what the maker specifies and not to whatever fits, and why gas supply problems go to Nicor Gas first.
