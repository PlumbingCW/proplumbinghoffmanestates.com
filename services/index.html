Not sure which one you need?

Many plumbing problems overlap. A slow drain can be a simple clog or a sign of a sewer line problem, and a high water bill can point to a hidden leak. Dispatch asks a few questions, then sends a licensed and insured plumber who finds the actual cause before any work starts.

Every job above is held to the Illinois Plumbing Code (77 Ill. Adm. Code 890) as the Village adopts it. A plumber licensed under 225 ILCS 320 is the standard to ask for.

You will hear the price before work begins. Same-day appointments are available when schedules allow, and the line is open 24/7 for emergencies.

What to do first in any plumbing emergency

  • Shut off the main water valve if water is running and will not stop.
  • Leave the building and call your gas utility or 911 if you smell gas.
  • Keep away from electrical equipment near standing water.
  • Take photos of damage for your records.
  • Call (630) 822-9290 and tell dispatch what you see.
